Salesforce announced three new editions in early September: Core, Advanced and Max. They replace the Enterprise, Unlimited and Agentforce 1 tiers, and each bundles Agentforce, Slack, Slackbot, embedded agentic analytics, data security and a Premier Success Plan. The editions differ in how many Flex Credits they include, with Core at 500,000, Advanced at 1M and Max at 2.75M. Existing Agentforce 1 customers can move to Max at no extra cost, with up to $500 in added value.

Per user per month, Core costs $195, Advanced $395 and Max $550. Measured against the editions they replace, Core rises $20 (11%) over Enterprise, Advanced rises $45 (13%) over Unlimited, and Max matches the former Agentforce 1 price. Salesforce claims added value of 70%, 50% and 60% respectively, although the author notes it is unclear how that figure is calculated. Salesforce says legacy customers' pricing is unchanged, which the author questions.

The author argues that bundling can justify the increase if your org already pays for Slack, Tableau and Premier Success, since a standalone Slack Business+ seat is about $180 a year and Premier Success adds roughly 30% to licence costs. Otherwise you may pay 11% more for products you do not use. The article suggests listing what you already use, matching the edition to your actual feature needs, and waiting for official pricing on agentic MCP and API calls and Industry Clouds before deciding if those will affect your choice.
