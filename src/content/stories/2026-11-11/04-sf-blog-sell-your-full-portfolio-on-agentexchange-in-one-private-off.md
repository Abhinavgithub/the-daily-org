Partners can now bring a Managed Package, partner-hosted SaaS products and professional services together in one private offer on AgentExchange. The offer can combine one-time service charges with recurring software or support subscriptions in a single order. Licenses for the Managed Package are provisioned automatically and kept in sync with the order, and the buyer receives one consolidated invoice.

Two further capabilities are added. Eligible partners can offer 24- or 36-month agreements with annual invoicing, though multi-year terms need account-level enablement from a Salesforce Partner Account Manager. Renewals can start from a pre-populated offer based on an existing order, with automated billing and license reconciliation at the contract anniversary and advance renewal notifications whose cadence is configured through Flow Builder.

Buyers must opt in to private offers in their Salesforce org before they can receive and accept them. The post points to release notes and a partner marketing hub for further detail.
