Salesforce has agreed to acquire Braze, an engagement platform that reacts to customer behaviour in real time across email, push, in-app messaging, SMS and WhatsApp, in a deal valued at around $5.66B. Salesforce expects to close during fiscal 2028, subject to shareholder and regulatory approval. Braze says in an SEC filing that its integration with Data 360, Customer 360 and Agentforce remains a possibility and that no decisions have been made.

The article notes that Salesforce has not published a combined roadmap showing how Braze's capabilities will overlap with Marketing Cloud Engagement, which relies on Journey Builder, and Marketing Cloud Next, which centres on Data 360, AI and Flow. Salesforce's president of applications and marketing suggested Braze would extend Marketing Cloud Next with real-time, mobile-first engagement rather than replace Marketing Cloud.

For practitioners, the piece argues that the greatest risk is over-specialising in one orchestration tool, and that event tracking, identity, API integration and data architecture skills will grow in value. It also expects consulting work around integration, migration and platform rationalisation, while warning that pricing, packaging and third-party integration openness remain unknown.
