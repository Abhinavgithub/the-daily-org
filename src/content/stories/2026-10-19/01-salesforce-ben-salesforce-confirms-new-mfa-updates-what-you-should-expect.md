Salesforce has clarified its ongoing multi-factor authentication rollout after conflicting messages caused confusion among customers. The company reaffirmed that mandatory multi-factor authentication remains required for all users by June 2026, with production enforcement beginning on July 20. Administrators and users with elevated permissions must use phishing-resistant methods.

Recent reports suggested enforcement might be delayed indefinitely or pushed past late October, but Salesforce explicitly stated there are no open-ended postponements. The platform is currently collaborating directly with a small group of customers who previously received extensions and require additional time to complete their security configurations.

A tool expected by mid-October will allow organizations to request ninety-day extensions for non-compliant users. Salesforce notes that over ninety percent of customers have already adopted the required controls and will contact any remaining accounts directly to ensure compliance within the next six to eight months.
