Salesforce announced that the Trailblazer Community forums will transition to a Slack workspace, with the new experience launching in November 2026. The existing forums will become read-only on October 30, 2026, halting new posts until the Slack environment goes live.

The archived forum content will remain searchable on the website, but new discussions will occur in Slack channels organized by region, role, and product. Accepted answers from designated product channels will be synced back to the web in early 2027, though they will be summarized and anonymized rather than preserving full conversation threads.

An AI Answers Agent will handle straightforward queries within Slack, while users can still connect with peers through tailored channels. Practitioners are advised to verify their Trailblazer ID email addresses, confirm workplace Slack access policies, and complete introductory Trailhead modules before the migration.
