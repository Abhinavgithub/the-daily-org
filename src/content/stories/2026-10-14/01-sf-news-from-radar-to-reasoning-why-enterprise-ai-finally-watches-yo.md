The article frames enterprise data challenges around the historical analogy of radar, noting that businesses currently lack a layer to make massive real-time data streams legible before action windows close. It introduces operational intelligence as the solution, moving beyond static dashboards toward continuous AI reasoning over live information.

Two specific models power this approach. Deep Insights processes structured business data to trace issues to their root causes and recommend subsequent steps. Moirai handles time-series forecasting by predicting demand and detecting anomalies while automatically incorporating known business variables such as seasonal shifts or renewal waves.

Salesforce is already deploying these capabilities internally. Engineering 360 uses Deep Insights to correlate patterns across ninety billion records, while the Contact Center division applies Moirai to optimize staffing and demand planning. The company plans to extend these reasoning layers across sales pipeline management, marketing performance tracking, and supply chain operations.
