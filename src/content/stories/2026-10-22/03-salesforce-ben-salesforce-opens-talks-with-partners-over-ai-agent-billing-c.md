Salesforce has introduced a pricing model requiring external artificial intelligence agents to register on the platform and consume Flex Credits for every successful call made through the Model Context Protocol or direct application programming interfaces. The initiative tracks each interaction as a Headless Platform Interaction, shifting costs away from traditional seat licensing toward usage-based billing for agentic traffic.

The announcement triggered significant pushback from implementation partners and independent architects who warned that per-action charges would create financial friction and discourage developers from building around the platform. Critics noted that customers already face heavy seat costs and questioned whether the market would accept paying for every individual request made by automated systems.

In response to the community reaction, Salesforce leadership initiated direct conversations with key ecosystem stakeholders to address these concerns. Participants emphasized the need for transparent pricing structures, additional platform value to justify the fees, and mechanisms that allow partners and administrators to continue leveraging artificial intelligence tools without unexpected production expenses.
