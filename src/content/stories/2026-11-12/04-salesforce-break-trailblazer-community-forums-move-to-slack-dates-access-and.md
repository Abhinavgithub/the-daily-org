The Trailblazer Community forums stopped accepting new posts on October 30, 2026, and the online community is moving to a Slack workspace that is now in open beta. Salesforce says the move is meant to support real-time conversation, help members find local Trailblazers and groups, and speed up answers through Slackbot and an Answers Agent. The full timeline runs from the September 15, 2026 announcement at Dreamforce through the November 2026 beta launch to early 2027, when accepted answers from Slack product channels begin syncing back to the community website.

The web forums now work as a read-only archive. Members can browse and search every historical discussion, and Salesforce says it will not remove any content. Joining is free: you sign in with your existing Trailblazer ID, so no paid Slack license or separate account is needed. If an employer blocks Slack, the article suggests using a personal device and the personal email tied to your TBID, or opening the workspace in a web browser.

Members who answered questions in the old forums may see a large unread count, because some forum data was synced into Slack with original authors attributed. The article gives a shortcut to clear it: open the workspace, press Shift + Esc to mark all messages as read, and confirm. Group pages stay on trailblazercommunitygroups.com, local groups gather in regional hubs such as #hub-northern-california, and global channels cover topics like Admins, Developers, Architects and Agentforce. Every channel is public, and members can request new ones after launch.
