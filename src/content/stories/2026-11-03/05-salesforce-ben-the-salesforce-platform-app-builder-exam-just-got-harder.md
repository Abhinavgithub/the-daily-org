The Certified Platform App Builder exam was refreshed on August 21, aligned to the Summer '26 release, and its pass rate moved from 63% to 73%. According to a podcast interview with Salesforce's Alexis Kingma, the largest change is depth on Flow. Candidates are now asked to choose the right flow type for a scenario, troubleshoot a broken flow, and maintain and monitor flows over time, rather than simply show they can build one.

Agentforce is also included, but framed as one automation tool within the app builder's toolkit rather than as a specialist topic. Candidates should recognize when it is an option and when reaching for it makes sense. Salesforce said the passing score was set using the Angoff method, in which a panel of experts estimates how many minimally qualified candidates would answer each question correctly. A higher passing score therefore means the panel expected a borderline candidate to get more questions right, which the author reads as a higher bar for the role.

The author advises budgeting time for Flow troubleshooting and monitoring, studying Agentforce under the Business Logic and Process Automation section (32% of the exam), using the Trailhead trail as a guide to likely topics, and reading the verbs in the exam guide objectives closely, since "create and configure" implies hands-on skill while "describe" implies conceptual knowledge.
