Salesforce announced AIforce at Dreamforce, a set of applications that connect platform data and workflows to Claude and Slack. The initial release has three parts: Claudeforce, Slackforce and Agentforce Coworker. Claudeforce comes out of an expanded partnership with Anthropic and ships with 37 pre-installed sales capabilities, from prospecting to opportunity management, plus a Claude Code development add-on with more than 40 further tools. Deloitte, GitLab and Legora piloted it, and it is now in beta for all customers.

Slackforce brings Salesforce data into Slack conversations, letting teams combine information from different sources, apply filters and take actions through interactive surfaces. The upgraded Slackbot assistant and Slack CRM feature can update files and create records from simple text commands.

Agentforce Coworker works inside Salesforce's internal Lightning environment as a digital assistant that uses customer history and activity. According to the company, it needs no data migration or new permission settings, and it recorded 100,000 activations in its first 35 days.
