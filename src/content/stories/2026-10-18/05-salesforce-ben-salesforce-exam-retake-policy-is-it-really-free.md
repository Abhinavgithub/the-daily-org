Salesforce and Pearson VUE are running a global promotion that waives the fee for one failed certification exam retake. To qualify, candidates must schedule, pay for, and complete their first attempt between September 29 and November 17, 2026. The promotion excludes Accredited Professional and CTA exams.

Claiming the voucher requires opting in through Pearson VUE to receive a unique code. Candidates must apply this code directly on Trailhead Academy during checkout, not on the general participating programs page. The retake must occur within a strict window from January 4 to April 7, 2027, and must match the original exam format, language, and delivery mode.

The financial benefit covers only the second attempt fee, effectively saving half the standard retake cost rather than the full price. Because the scheduled window includes holiday periods and forces a minimum seven-week gap after a late November failure, the author advises using the offer strategically only when already prepared to sit for a difficult exam without urgent hiring deadlines.
