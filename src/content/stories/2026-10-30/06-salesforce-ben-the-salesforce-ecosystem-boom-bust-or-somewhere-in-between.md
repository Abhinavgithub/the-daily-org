Salesforce itself continues to grow. Its Q2 FY27 revenue reached $11.3B, up 11% year on year, with a $456M Informatica contribution, and Agentforce and Data 360 annual recurring revenue approached $3.9B. Even so, IDC shows Salesforce's CRM market share slipping to 20% in 2025 from 20.7% in 2024.

The 10K Salesforce Ecosystem Report for 2026 counts a record 4,247 consulting partners, up 14%. Growth sits almost entirely among small firms with five or fewer certified experts, while every consulting tier above ten experts shrank. Global demand for Salesforce talent fell 13% year on year, though the authors say demand for AI, data and revenue management skills grew sharply while generalist skills dropped.

The author's Salary Survey 2026 points the same way: 89.4% of respondents found the market harder than in past years, and among those who changed jobs, 41.6% reported lower pay against 22.6% who reported higher. The conclusion is that the ecosystem is changing shape, with growth concentrated in specialist areas and pressure on generalist roles, large firms and individual bargaining power.
