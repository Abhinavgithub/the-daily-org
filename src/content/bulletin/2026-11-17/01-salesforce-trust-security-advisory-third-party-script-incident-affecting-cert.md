Salesforce is investigating unauthorized modifications to scripts supplied by Optimizely and used on certain Salesforce websites. The altered scripts could load malicious code in visitors' browsers. As a precaution, Salesforce has confirmed that Optimizely scripts are disabled across preview and production instances, and it will notify affected customers without undue delay if it finds unauthorized access to their data.
