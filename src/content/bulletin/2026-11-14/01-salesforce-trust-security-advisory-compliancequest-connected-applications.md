Salesforce has disabled all ComplianceQuest apps and associated managed packages as a precaution after spotting suspicious use of access tokens linked to the third-party provider. Salesforce says this was a protective measure rather than a platform outage or a vulnerability in the core Salesforce platform, and that the two companies are investigating together. The integrations will stay unavailable until ComplianceQuest completes its security remediation and shows they can be restored safely. Affected customers should contact ComplianceQuest at info@compliancequest.com with questions, and will be told without undue delay if unauthorized access to their data is found. The notice also records that the managed packages were re-enabled following an earlier advisory, so customers who were affected there should see functionality return, and should contact Salesforce Support if problems continue.
