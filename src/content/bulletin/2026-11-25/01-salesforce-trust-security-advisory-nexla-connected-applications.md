Salesforce has disabled connections from the Nexla Connected App to customer instances as a precaution, after identifying suspicious activity associated with that connected application. The action was a security measure to protect Salesforce customers, not the result of a platform service disruption or a vulnerability in the core Salesforce platform.

Customers using Nexla services should contact Nexla directly for questions about those services and the ongoing remediation work. Salesforce and Nexla are working together to restore the affected integrations safely, but they will stay unavailable until Nexla completes its security remediation and shows that the integrations can be restored without risk. Salesforce says it will notify affected customers without undue delay if unauthorized access to their data is identified.
