A Core Service disruption began on October 23, causing login failures and internal server errors across sixty-two Salesforce instances. Affected users are unable to access their orgs while engineering teams roll back a conflicting application update and deploy a fix forward. Users should monitor the official Trust page for 30 minute updates and await instance-specific recovery notifications.
