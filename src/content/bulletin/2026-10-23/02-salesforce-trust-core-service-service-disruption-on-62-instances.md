A Core Service disruption began on October 23, when a recent release stopped login sessions from being created, leaving users on eighty-six Salesforce instances unable to reach their orgs. Salesforce reported it as three incidents as groups of instances recovered at different times, and deployed a fix forward to restore access on all of them. No action is needed from users; the Trust site will carry the findings of the investigation.
