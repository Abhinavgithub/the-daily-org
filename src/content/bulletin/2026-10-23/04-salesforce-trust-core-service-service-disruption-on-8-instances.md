A recent release update introduced a defect that prevented users from creating login sessions, causing a service disruption across eight instances starting on October 23. The issue has been resolved after a fix was deployed forward, restoring full access to orgs. Administrators and developers should monitor the Trust site for updates regarding the full technical investigation and preventive measures.
