A recent release introduced a bug that prevented login sessions from being created across sixteen Core Service instances. The outage began on October 23, 2026, and impacted all users attempting to access their Salesforce orgs. Salesforce deployed a forward fix to restore access, and users should now be able to log in normally without further intervention.
