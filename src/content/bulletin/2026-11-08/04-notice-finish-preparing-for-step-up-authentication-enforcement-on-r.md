To help protect your org's data from unauthorized exfiltration, Salesforce requires users to complete a step-up authentication challenge when they export reports. This release update applies only to orgs that received an extension for step-up authentication enforcement.

**What to do:** Implement step-up authentication challenges for all report exports before Spring ’27.
