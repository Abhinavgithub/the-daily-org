To help protect your org's most privileged accounts from phishing and account takeover risks, Salesforce requires phishing-resistant multi-factor authentication (MFA) for users with the System Administrator profile or privileged permissions. This release update applies only to orgs that received an extension for phishing-resistant MFA enforcement.

**What to do:** Configure phishing-resistant multi-factor authentication for system administrators before Spring ’27.
