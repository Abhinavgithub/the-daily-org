To help protect your org from security threats such as phishing, credential stuffing, and account takeovers, Salesforce requires multi-factor authentication (MFA) for all employee logins, including direct UI and Single Sign-On (SSO). This release update applies only to orgs that received an extension for MFA enforcement.

**What to do:** Enable multi-factor authentication for all employee logins before Spring ’27 enforcement.
