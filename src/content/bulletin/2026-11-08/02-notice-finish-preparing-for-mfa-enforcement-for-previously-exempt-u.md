To help protect your org from security threats such as phishing, credential stuffing, and account takeovers, Salesforce requires multi-factor authentication (MFA) for all employee logins, including direct UI and single sign-on (SSO). This release update applies only to orgs that received an extension to use the “Waive Multi-Factor Authentication for Exempt Users” permission.

**What to do:** Remove the waive multi-factor authentication permission and enforce MFA for exempt users.
