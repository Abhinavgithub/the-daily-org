Salesforce says it is aware of and actively investigating two recently disclosed NGINX vulnerabilities, CVE-2026-123633 and CVE-2026-123007, which allow unauthenticated remote code execution. The company states that it has no evidence these flaws have been exploited against Salesforce or affected customer data.

Salesforce is handling the issue under its established vulnerability management processes. If it finds evidence of impact to customer data, or determines that customers need to take action in their Salesforce environments, it will notify affected customers without undue delay. Until then, the notice does not ask customers to make any changes.
