A resolved service disruption impacted twenty-six Marketing Cloud instances, preventing logins and database-dependent features for Marketing Cloud Engagement customers. Administrators and developers can resume normal operations after Salesforce restarted the affected database servers, validated recovery, and rebalanced the environment. Users should monitor the Trust page for updates on the ongoing investigation into the underlying cause.
