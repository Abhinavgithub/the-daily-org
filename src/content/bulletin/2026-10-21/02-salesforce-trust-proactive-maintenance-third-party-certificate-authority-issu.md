Salesforce has identified a publicly reported compromise involving intermediate certificates issued by a third-party certificate authority. The company is actively monitoring the situation and may execute proactive maintenance to mitigate risks across its platform. Administrators and architects should monitor the Salesforce Trust status page for ongoing updates and prepare for potential service interruptions during maintenance windows.
