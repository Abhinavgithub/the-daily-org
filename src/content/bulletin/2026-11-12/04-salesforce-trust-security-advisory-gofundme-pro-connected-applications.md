Salesforce has disabled the GoFundMe Pro Connected App and its associated managed packages as a precaution, after it found suspicious use of access tokens linked to the third-party provider. Salesforce says this was a security measure to protect customers, not a service disruption, and that the cause was not a vulnerability in the core Salesforce platform. Salesforce and GoFundMe are investigating together, and the integrations will stay unavailable until the security remediation is complete and GoFundMe has shown they can be restored safely. Salesforce says it will notify affected customers without undue delay if it finds unauthorized access to their data, and customers with questions about GoFundMe services or remediation should contact GoFundMe directly.
