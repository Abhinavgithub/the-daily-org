Salesforce disabled connections from the RB2B Connected App to customer instances as a precaution after identifying suspicious activity associated with the app itself. The action was a security measure, not a platform outage or a vulnerability in the core Salesforce platform, and questions about RB2B services and remediation should go to RB2B directly. Salesforce has since re-enabled the RB2B Connected Apps following RB2B's security remediation, so functionality for affected customers should be restored. Anyone still experiencing issues should contact Salesforce Support, and Salesforce says it will notify affected customers without undue delay if unauthorized access to their data is identified.
