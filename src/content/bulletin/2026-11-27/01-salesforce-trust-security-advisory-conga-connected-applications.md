Salesforce has disabled connections from its customers' instances to the Conga Connected App and associated managed packages as a precaution. The step followed suspicious activity involving Conga's Connected App credential store. Salesforce says this was a security measure, not a platform outage or a vulnerability in the core Salesforce platform.

The integrations will stay unavailable until Conga completes its security remediation and shows they can be restored safely. Salesforce and Conga are working together to bring them back as soon as possible. Salesforce also says it will notify affected customers without undue delay if unauthorized access to their data is found.

Affected customers should contact Conga directly about Conga services and the remediation effort. The notice links to a Salesforce blog post explaining how it responds to Connected App and managed package incidents.
