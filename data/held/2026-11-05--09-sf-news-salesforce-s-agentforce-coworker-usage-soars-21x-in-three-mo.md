Salesforce reports that more than 185,000 users are working with Agentforce Coworker, with usage up 21x in three months. The release adds Conversation History, reusable Skills and scheduled tasks, plus connections to Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 so that email, calendar and file data can sit alongside Salesforce records. Coworker can be used from Microsoft Teams and new iOS, Mac and Windows apps.

Coworker plans multi-step jobs, coordinates Agentforce agents, flows and business actions, and returns results for review. It is described as working within existing Sharing Rules, permissions and configured access controls, with approval and audit capabilities for supported operations. A new $50 per-user, per-month option is offered for predictable budgeting.

Availability is staged: Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 connectivity, Conversation History, Skills and the per-user SKU are available now; the Teams, iOS and desktop apps and a Coworker MCP server are slated for November 2026; Daily Digest, scheduled tasks and mobile apps for Field Service, Consumer Goods Cloud and Life Sciences Cloud are expected in December 2026. The article notes pricing and features may change, and much of the piece is customer testimony and marketing framing.
