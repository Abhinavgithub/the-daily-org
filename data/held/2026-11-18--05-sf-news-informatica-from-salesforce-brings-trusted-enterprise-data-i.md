Informatica from Salesforce has expanded its collaboration with Microsoft, announced at Microsoft Ignite 2026. The integration brings Informatica's enterprise data intelligence into Microsoft Copilot and Teams, giving business users access to business definitions, data quality, governance and lineage through natural-language experiences. The article presents this as a response to the gap between agents that can access data and agents that understand it and operate within access policies.

For AI developers, the integration with Microsoft Foundry extends Informatica Headless MCP servers, which reached general availability earlier, with new governance, identity and administrative capabilities. These let developers ground agents in authoritative definitions, quality indicators and lineage, apply access controls, and trace agent interactions with data management services. Informatica's MCP services are also being expanded across Azure-based IDMC points of delivery worldwide.

For data engineers and analysts on Fabric, Informatica's scanners now cover notebooks and data pipelines as well as data at rest. The enhanced scanners provide lineage across multistep pipeline orchestrations, support impact analysis, and are administered through a consolidated interface. The article also announces a new U.S. East Coast Azure point of delivery for customers with data residency or procurement requirements, and notes that Informatica purchases through Microsoft Marketplace can count toward a Microsoft Azure Consumption Commitment.

Availability is staggered: the Copilot integration is generally available, the Foundry MCP server integration is generally available with its expanded capabilities, the Fabric scanners are generally available for notebooks and pipelines, and the Azure U.S. East IDMC point of delivery is generally available. The piece is largely promotional, with customer and executive quotes, so the technical detail on the MCP governance features is thin.
