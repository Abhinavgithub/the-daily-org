The Spring 2026 Agentforce Specialist exam has been restructured, removing separate Sales Cloud and Service Cloud sections while adding Agent Script, hybrid reasoning, Testing Center workflows, governance, and multi agent orchestration. The assessment consists of 60 scored multiple choice questions plus five unscored ones, lasts 105 minutes, requires a 72 percent passing score, and costs 200 dollars to register.

A four week preparation strategy emphasizes practical application over rote memorization. Week one focuses on prompt engineering and the Trust Layer using field generation and flex templates grounded in record data. Week two covers Data Cloud fundamentals, guiding readers to build a file based Data Library and connect policy documents to test retrieval behavior. Week three shifts to agent creation, requiring the construction of an agent with Flow backed actions and deterministic decision filters. The final week covers the Testing Center, deployment procedures, analytics, and governance monitoring.

Common study mistakes include relying on outdated outlines, underweighting the AI Agents section, treating Data Cloud as optional, and ignoring annual maintenance requirements that expire the certification. The article concludes that demonstrating configured solutions and tested agents during technical interviews carries more weight than the certification badge alone.
