Salesforce has expanded Agentforce Coworker, an AI teammate that takes delegated work, plans the steps, coordinates agents and returns results for review. The article lists the update: Microsoft Teams integration, Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 connectivity, reusable Skills, Conversation History, iOS, Mac and Windows apps, and a Coworker MCP server that lets it be used from Claude and ChatGPT.

The main change for buyers is pricing. The standalone Coworker licence is $50 per user per month, including all Coworker searches and 30 MB of indexing per user per month. The article compares this with the $5 basic Agentforce User Licence plus Flex Credits, which are priced at $500 per 100,000 credits or $2 per conversation, and notes that the $50 sits on top of existing Salesforce cloud licences.

The article describes a renewal-at-risk example and the pattern of delegate, coordinate and review. It also notes that scheduled tasks and a Daily Digest are due in December 2026, that the adoption figures are self-reported by Salesforce, and that no independent benchmark of delegated-work quality exists yet.
