Salesforce has announced that Salesforce Services on Hyperforce now runs on Google Cloud infrastructure. The environment has completed its first round of security compliance assessments and certifications, covering SOC 1, SOC 2 and SOC 3, PCI DSS, ISO 27001, ISO 27017, ISO 27018 and NEN 7510. Salesforce says customers keep the Salesforce experience they already know, with the same security and compliance baseline as its other Hyperforce environments.

The post explains what each certification is meant to give customers. The SOC reports cover the controls behind the services, PCI DSS covers safeguards for payment card data, the ISO standards address information security, cloud security and personal data privacy, and NEN 7510 addresses information security in healthcare. Salesforce presents these as independent checks that customers can use to see how their data is protected.

Salesforce describes this as the start of an effort to achieve certification parity across its Salesforce Services environments. It says it will keep commissioning independent assessments so that customers can build on Hyperforce on Google Cloud with the same confidence they have elsewhere. The post points readers to the Salesforce Compliance site for the full reports and to a help article for which orgs sit in which location and which Hyperforce regions are available.
